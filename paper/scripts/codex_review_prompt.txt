What to evaluate
================================================================

CONTENT
- Overclaims and underclaims, especially around novelty (this is a method
  paper that should not oversell against HAWQ/CLADO/ParoQuant/IMPQ/AMQ).
- Mathematical rigor: section 3 (theory) and the "monotone non-regression"
  proposition. Are the definitions tight? Are the dependencies between
  L1/L2/L3 stated correctly?
- Accuracy of cited prior work: do the descriptions of HAWQ-V3, CLADO,
  GPTQ, kneedle (Satopaa 2011), ORD (Ortega/Ramchandran 1998), and
  the microscaling spec match the actual content of those references?
  Flag anything that mischaracterizes a prior work.
- Empirical numbers: cross-check every numeric claim against handovers,
  run dirs, and the HF model card. Flag any number that doesn't have a
  ground-truth source.
- Tool-eval-bench reporting: the paper goes out of its way to be honest
  about TC-42 / TC-60. Is the framing fair, or does it under-report a
  meaningful regression?
- Code-paper drift: does anything the paper claims correspond to a code
  path that's actually default-ON, default-OFF, or removed?
- Reproducibility: is the appendix detailed enough that an outsider could
  re-run the pipeline?
- Section 5 ("Methods Considered and Rejected"): does each entry cite the
  specific claim faithfully? Did the paper get the empirical justification
  right for each rejection?

FORMATTING / TYPESETTING
- LaTeX style: awkward typesetting, broken refs, ugly hyphenation,
  inconsistent capitalization in section headers, table layout.
- Mathematical typography: \operatorname vs \mathrm usage, theorem
  environments, equation numbering, vector/matrix conventions.
- Figures: open figures in the rendered PDF (use pdftotext or describe
  via the PDF). For each figure, is it: legible, well-captioned, properly
  referenced in text, structurally informative? Are any figures redundant
  or aesthetically weak?
- Tables: column alignment, units, missing entries, captions.
- Bibliography: entries that should have first-author full names, missing
  volume/page numbers, inconsistent venue formatting (some use just arXiv,
  some have proper venue), missing DOIs where they should appear.
- Title and abstract: is the title accurate? Does the abstract front-load
  the contribution clearly?
- Length: 20 pages with 13 figures; right-sized for a method paper, or
  bloated/under-developed somewhere?
